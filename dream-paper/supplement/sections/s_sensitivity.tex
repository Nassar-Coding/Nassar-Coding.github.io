\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Priority weights equalized}: policy ordering changes ---
the point-forecast greedy policy falls behind proportional allocation in
New York, while quantile-greedy stays within 0.6--4.2\% of the
perfect-information reference everywhere. Priorities are normative
inputs; their influence on the ranking is therefore reported, not
averaged away.
\item \textbf{10\% daily backlog abandonment}: ordering of all policies
unchanged in all four cities.
\item \textbf{Lagged-weather variant (A3 removed)}: removes most of the
target-day weather gain (New York 165.4 $\rightarrow$ 178.6 against a
182.4 calendar-only reference); conclusions about weather are therefore
stated as conditional on day-ahead forecast availability.
\item \textbf{Capacity grid}: the 70/90/110\% regimes are the central
sensitivity axis of the paper and all results are reported per regime.
\end{itemize}
