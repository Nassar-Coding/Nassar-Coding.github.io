\paragraph{Reproducibility statement.}
Every number, table, and figure in this paper regenerates from the
versioned raw data with one command (\texttt{make all}) in the companion
repository, which contains the acquisition scripts with exact query URLs,
SHA-256 manifests for every raw file, the frozen-budget and
pooled-censoring proof manifests, deterministic seeds, and the
16-guard/37-test suite of Section~5 that fails the build on any protocol
violation.
