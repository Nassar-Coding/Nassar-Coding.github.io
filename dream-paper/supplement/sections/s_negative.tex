The corrected protocol produced several results that resolved against the
more optimistic hypothesis; all are retained as findings:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Weather adds nothing in Austin}: $+0.001$ test MAE on top
of calendar features (block-bootstrap interval covers zero), against a
9.3\% gain in New York.
\item \textbf{Pooled training hurts under valid censoring}: with
stage-censored pooled fits (Amendment C11), pooling raises test MAE
significantly in Austin ($+4.1\%$), Chicago ($+6.5\%$), and New York
($+1.8\%$); San Francisco is neutral. Censored zero-shot transfer
degrades MAE by $+5\%$ to $+149\%$.
\item \textbf{The exact point-forecast optimizer loses to proportional
apportionment} in all 12 city-regime settings under the neutral
objective --- the identified mechanism being that a degenerate forecast
makes the marginal-value profile piecewise flat in \emph{both}
segments ($\kappa\omega$ below the point estimate plus carryover, zero
above), so the greedy ordering is tie-broken rather than informed;
distributional tails restore an allocation gradient (worked examples
for both segments in the optimality section).
\item \textbf{Quantile intervals are under-dispersed}: 90\% nominal,
76.8--81.2\% empirical; unrepaired in this paper.
\item \textbf{A protocol defect was detected and corrected during review}
(R1-F1): per-city split boundaries differ by 1--2 calendar days, so
uncensored pooled fits contained $\approx$0.1\% contemporaneous
other-city rows. After the targeted censoring fix, the
selection-experiment story changed from 7/12 disagreements with 4
apparent harms to 9/12 agreements, 2 gains, 0 harms --- the earlier
disagreement was substantially an artifact of the contaminated pooled
model's inflated validation scores. The pre-fix outputs are preserved in
the repository history for audit.
\item \textbf{The hindsight myopic reference is not a bound}: being
per-day myopic under carryover it can be exceeded; it appears only in
the diagnostic table below and is never used as a denominator.
\end{itemize}
