\paragraph{Stability.} All H1 comparisons hold in each of five
expanding-window rolling-origin folds per city, and all headline
forecast differences carry day-block bootstrap $p$-values at or below
0.0015 except the Austin weather null ($p=0.995$) and the San
Francisco/Austin pooling effects (not significant).

\paragraph{Decision-layer sensitivity.} Sweeps over the stylization
parameters preserve the qualitative conclusions: with 10\% daily backlog
abandonment, policy ordering is unchanged in all cities; with equalized
priority weights, the uniform floor worsens dramatically (weighted
losses scale differently) while the quantile-greedy policy stays within
0.6--4.2\% of the perfect-information reference in every city. The
capacity grid itself (70/90/110\%) is the primary sensitivity axis and
is reported in full rather than averaged.

\paragraph{What would change the conclusions.} The decision results are
conditional on the stylization assumptions A4--A6 (interchangeable
crews, fixed productivity, no intra-day dynamics); we claim regime
\emph{patterns}, not city-specific magnitudes. The forecast results are
unconditional on those assumptions. The weather findings are conditional
on A3 (day-ahead forecast availability), bounded by the lagged-weather
variant.
